# Questions for the lawyer

Collected by /launch-kit. Deduplicated; 15 items (the cap). Not legal advice; send this list to counsel, not the site.

1. Should the Motiv name be changed? Does UK journal entry 2025/046 (Class 9, AI meeting transcription) or USPTO serial 79424201 (MOTIV, Class 9) block the name in the US, EU, or UK?
2. Is "Motiv" too close to MOTIVE (Motive Technologies, Motive Software Solutions) in sound and class to launch under that name?
3. Should we file MOTIV in Classes 9 and 42 in the US, EU, and UK before sales open? Can we use Emruz for consulting (Classes 35 and 42)? The logo files carry AI-tool provenance metadata: can an AI-generated logo be trademarked, and who owns it?
4. Is the legal party correct? The code names "Shahin Pirooz, doing business as Emruz" in Santa Clara, California. Should an LLC be the contracting party for Motiv sales and managed services?
5. Is the refund rule (full refund within 14 days of first purchase) enforceable as written? Do EU and UK customers keep a statutory withdrawal right for digital content that overrides it?
6. The Lifetime plan says "Premium for as long as you use Motiv" on the pricing page and "as long as Emruz offers Motiv" in the terms. Which wording do we commit to, and is a lifetime promise enforceable as written?
7. Governing law is California, with venue in Santa Clara County. Does that hold for EU and UK consumers, who keep local consumer protections?
8. Does selling Motiv to EU consumers bring in the European Accessibility Act? Do the WCAG 2.2 AA checks (contrast, keyboard, focus) need to be completed before launch?
9. Does the Motiv desktop app's daily check-in, and any auto-update or telemetry pings, need separate disclosure in the Motiv privacy policy? Does the EULA match what the app sends?
10. Do Netlify, Stripe, and Resend need signed data processing agreements for EU, UK, and California customers? Is the WhiteDog Cyber relationship (managed products) a processor or a separate controller? Is email-only account deletion and data export (no in-app route) enough under GDPR Art. 17 and 20, and CCPA?
11. Are the marketing claims acceptable as written: "secure and cost-effective from day one" on the home page, and "Premium never expires" on the pricing page?
12. The EspecialKay font sets the Emruz wordmark on every page, and no licence for it is in the repo. Is it licensed for web and commercial use? Who owns the Motiv icon artwork? Is the Meetily Community Edition MIT licence correctly disclosed in the Motiv third-party notices?
13. Does Stripe Checkout's renewal disclosure meet California's auto-renewal law, given that the site now states renewal on the plan notes? Is online cancellation (account page) enough?
14. Does Emruz need an EU or UK representative (GDPR Art. 27, UK GDPR equivalent) for Motiv sales to EU and UK customers? Does the CCPA apply, given the revenue and data-volume thresholds?
15. For assessment clients, is Emruz a processor for the evidence files they upload, and does a DPIA need to be done for evidence uploads, which may hold personal data of client staff?
