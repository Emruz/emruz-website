## Overall rating

- **Motiv: caution.** No confirmed live conflict, but at least one Class 9 "Motiv" mark (serial 79424201) and a UK journal entry for AI meeting transcription must be opened and checked before launch. Motive (fleet AI) and MOTIVE (Motive Software Solutions) are close in sound and class.
- **Emruz: clear, as far as this search reached.** No exact-name mark or company found in the results.

## Recommendation

1. **Before selling Motiv:** open the UK journal entry 2025/046 and USPTO serial 79424201 on TSDR. If either is a live "Motiv" for AI meeting software, rename the product or get counsel's opinion before launch. Renaming is cheaper now than after sales start.
2. **If Motiv stays:** file a trademark for MOTIV in Classes 9 and 42 in the US, EU, and UK, after the search above is complete. Filing requires an attorney's opinion on conflicts.
3. **Emruz:** consider filing in Classes 35 and 42 (and 45 if advisory) in the US, since the search found no exact-name conflict. Confirm the entity owner first.
4. **Use ™, not ®,** until a registration issues.
